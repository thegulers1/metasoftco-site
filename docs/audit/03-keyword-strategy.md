# Keyword, search intent and page strategy

No search volume, rank, traffic or difficulty numbers are asserted. Priorities are based on commercial relevance, fit with demonstrated capability, SERP composition and funnel role. Validate with GSC, Google Ads Keyword Planner and a paid SEO dataset before final forecasting.

## Search principles

- Own a small number of authoritative commercial hubs rather than 36 near-product rental pages.
- Use “AI event activation” and “experiential technology” to frame the business; use “AI photo booth” as the strongest product-entry cluster.
- Separate buyer intent from format names: brand activation, trade show, product launch, retail and corporate event searches need outcome/use-case pages, not copied service text.
- Preserve Turkish rental terms where they reflect real buying behavior; English global pages should not default to awkward “Rental” in every H1.
- A page becomes indexable only when MetasoftCo can show a real service, media, operating information and proof.

## Cannibalization and consolidation

### AI Photo / local pages

Potential overlap exists among:

- `/hizmetler/yapay-zeka-etkinlik-cozumleri/ai-photobooth-kirala`
- `/hizmetler/istanbul-ai-photobooth`
- `/sektorel-cozumler/istanbul-ai-photobooth`
- `/en/services/ai-event-solutions/ai-photobooth`
- `/en/sector-solutions/istanbul-ai-photobooth` (broken)

Keep one Turkish national/commercial AI Photobooth page and, only if local evidence/demand supports it, one Istanbul landing page with genuinely local logistics, venues/areas, service availability and cases. Remove the duplicate sector implementation and its broken English alternate. Do not create district doorway pages.

### Software sector pages

`/sektorel-yazilim-cozumleri/*` competes for a broad custom-software identity that is weakly connected to the experiential activation goal. Two English hubs also share the title “Industry-Specific Software Digital Transformation.”

Decision gate:

- if custom enterprise software is a distinct revenue line with portfolio/team/sales process, separate it as a clear business capability and deepen it;
- if not, noindex/consolidate the generic textile, health, food, automotive, finance, retail and SaaS pages into experiential use cases that MetasoftCo can prove.

Do not let broad “digital transformation” pages dilute the desired experiential entity.

### Service variants

Many individual formats target `[NAME] Kirala` with similar descriptions. Keep detail pages indexable only when they have distinct demand/intent and at least: unique guest journey, constraints, media, case, FAQs, and internal links. Otherwise combine them into a richer category gallery and let the formats be sections, not pages.

## Editorial quality rules

- One page, one primary intent; related terminology belongs in supporting sections.
- Lead with what it is, who it is for, and the event/business outcome.
- Use technology names where they answer a buyer question, not as decoration.
- Every commercial page needs a real visual, one case, guest journey, operating facts, privacy/data answer and contextual CTA.
- Never publish a translated page that falls back to Turkish.
- Mark verified metrics with scope/date; never convert estimates into claims.
- Add named author/reviewer and last-updated date for technical/privacy content.

## Measurement plan

Connect GSC by country/page/query and monitor:

- non-brand clicks/impressions for the six priority commercial clusters;
- indexed canonical URLs by locale;
- CTR/title tests on high-impression pages;
- organic landing page → qualified brief conversion;
- service/case assisted conversions;
- branded versus non-branded query mix;
- English query geography and lead quality.

Add rank/volume estimates only after exporting reliable first-party and paid-tool data.
