# Content, services, projects, trust and AEO audit

## Content diagnosis

The strongest content asset is the work itself. The weakest pattern is that pages often use general claims—innovative, interactive, AI-powered, unforgettable—where a buyer needs concrete experience flow, production constraints and evidence.

Preferred sentence model:

> capability + actual guest use + brand customization + operational fact + business value + evidence

Example:

> Guests take a portrait at a branded kiosk, choose one of the approved campaign worlds, and receive a moderated AI image by QR or print. The interface, physical set and final asset carry the campaign identity; participation and opt-in data can be reported after the event. See the Tavuk Dünyası case.

Every operational and performance statement must be verified by the team.

## Service portfolio audit

### AI event solutions

Current formats include AI Photo, AI Draw, AI Fashion Mirror, AI Greenbox, AI Bookmark, AI Star, Football Card, AI Style and child-oriented variants.

Opportunity: this is the clearest flagship capability. Reframe the category around business/guest outcomes and separate reusable experience engines from campaign concepts. “Forbes cover,” “star meetup,” “football card” and bookmark may be concepts powered by AI portrait/content systems, not equal top-level products.

Required facts by page:

- capture method and guest choices
- brand/prompt/style approval process
- identity/garment fidelity and moderation
- typical processing range under defined conditions
- queue/concurrency plan
- delivery: QR/email/SMS/print
- data fields, consent, retention and deletion
- footprint, power, internet and staff
- fallback operation
- accessible alternatives
- one real project

### Photobooth and photo activations

Current formats include standard photobooth, cabin, strip, mirror, glow box, collage, magazine cover, aura, Momento Ball, AR photo and background selection.

Issue: the catalogue reads as rental inventory. Consolidate visually or conceptually similar formats; distinguish each only by guest experience, physical form, output and buyer use case. One authoritative category page should explain branded photography as content/lead generation, not only taking pictures.

### Interactive games and activities

Current formats include gift wheel, memory/reflex/recycling/catch/puzzle games, drawing robot, graffiti wall, charge bike, karaoke and star map.

Issue: unrelated formats share one generic template and similar “Rental” titles. Reorganize by objective:

- attract/footfall
- compete/leaderboard
- learn/brand message
- sustainability/behavior
- reward/lead capture
- create/share

Show game loop, session duration, throughput, display/space needs, branding, scoring/reporting and spectator experience.

### Video, VR/AR, hologram, neuro/data, installations and software

Several category hubs are thin (~140–180 rendered words) and default to the site description. This makes the portfolio look speculative.

Publish these as indexable capability pages only when there are real projects, media, technical scope and a defined offer. Otherwise combine them under “Custom Interactive Installations” and let individual capabilities be supported sections. Avoid promising neuro/data experiences without ethical methodology, consent and proof.

### Case data collection form

Collect at project close:

- client and approving contact
- campaign/event name; venue/city/country; dates/duration
- agency/production partners and correct credit
- objective and target audience
- stations, team, hours and setup footprint
- number of interactions/unique participants/generated assets/prints/shares/opt-ins where reliably measured
- peak throughput, average generation time and queues, with method
- moderation/rejections/fallback events
- approved client quote
- final UI captures, physical setup, participant use, outputs, wide event footage
- usage/press/award rights
- data retention/deletion confirmation

## Trust and enterprise credibility

### Present

- recognizable client logos and project names
- Istanbul Teknokent address, phone and email
- project media
- claimed operating history, event and brand counts
- legal/privacy pages in Turkish
- technology vocabulary that suggests in-house capability

### Missing or underdeveloped

- team/leadership and production roles
- case metrics/methodology
- testimonials with name/role/company/permission
- deployment geography and partner model
- security/privacy summary for event images and leads
- data processor/subprocessor, retention and deletion practices
- brand-safety/content-moderation process
- on-site technical support and escalation
- equipment redundancy, offline/fallback and pre-event testing
- certifications/insurance only where real
- press/awards only where real

Enterprise trust module for service/case pages:

```text
Built, tested and operated by MetasoftCo
• Campaign-approved visual styles and interface
• On-site operator and pre-event testing
• Consent-led capture and defined deletion workflow
• Backup/fallback plan agreed before show day
• Post-event participation/reporting where configured
```

Publish only statements backed by the operating process.

## Copy quality and translation

High-risk patterns:

- visible H1s include `| MetasoftCo` and full SEO-title syntax;
- English titles retain “Yapay Zeka,” “İnteraktif,” and “Fotoğraf”;
- “Sectoral” is used where native English would say industry-specific;
- default Turkish descriptions appear on English pages;
- “Rental” is appended mechanically to custom brand activations;
- technical names such as LoRA/ControlNet appear before buyer meaning;
- blanket claims such as “99%” and “30 days” lack visible scope/method.

Native English terminology:

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| Sectoral software solutions | Industry-specific software / solutions for [use case] |
| Photography activities | Branded photo experiences / photo activations |
| Interactive activities | Interactive event games and experiences |
| AI Photo rental | AI photo activation / AI photo booth for events |
| Get offer | Request a proposal / Plan your activation |
| Stage in 30 days | From approved concept to live deployment in [verified range] |

## Blog and thought leadership

The blog currently behaves as a small isolated publication. Article pages expose navigation/footer but little contextual service/project linking. The strongest technical article has two H1s and inconsistent “400” versus “500” participant claims across languages/heading copy.

Recommended article template:

- answer-first summary
- who the advice is for
- factual sections with diagrams/photos
- “what this means for an event brief”
- relevant case module
- related capability
- named author/reviewer and updated date
- contextual CTA

Do not chase generic AI news. Publish procurement-grade material that a brand manager or agency producer can use.

## AEO / generative-search readiness

### Current positives

- service definitions, FAQs and structured data exist;
- company address and capability terms are explicit;
- `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` are publicly available;
- projects link recognizable brands to activation names.

### Gaps

- entity definition varies between digital agency, software application, local business and activation agency;
- global schema overstates/misclassifies the entity;
- facts are not consistently sourced or scoped;
- case relationships/results are unstructured;
- English content may be Turkish fallback;
- authorship/editorial review is weak;
- broken schema logo harms entity coherence;
- important category hubs are orphaned.

### Recommended factual blocks

On About/Home, answer concisely:

- What is MetasoftCo?
- What does it build?
- Who buys it?
- Where does it operate?
- What is handled in-house?
- How are event images/data handled?
- How can agencies work with it?

Use consistent names and `@id` references. AEO value comes from clear, corroborated entities and evidence, not additional “AI SEO” pages.

## Content governance

Before a record can be published:

- required native-language title, slug, H1, summary, body and metadata
- canonical/alternate pair validation
- no null/duplicate slug
- valid JSON gallery/FAQ/specs with schema validation
- at least one optimized media asset and useful alt
- assigned primary intent/keyword cluster
- one related service/case and one contextual CTA
- claims/evidence approval
- preview at 320/390/1440
- link/status/schema checks pass

Content owner: marketing/content. Technical gate owner: engineering. Claim approval: operations/account lead. Native English approval: professional editor with experiential/B2B familiarity.
