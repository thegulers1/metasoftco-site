# MetasoftCo Phase 2 — Strategy and Three-Page Prototype

Date: 14 August 2026

Scope: English homepage, AI Photo Booth service, Tavuk Dünyası AI Photo case study

Status: Prototype implemented; site-wide rollout intentionally not started

## Executive Summary

The audit recommendation remains the strongest positioning: **AI-powered experiential technology studio**. It describes MetasoftCo’s demonstrated mix of creative direction, custom software, AI image systems, physical installation and event delivery more accurately than either “software company” or “photo booth rental.” It also leaves room to grow beyond events without obscuring the immediate commercial offer.

The prototype replaces an abstract, effect-led presentation with a work-first editorial system. The three pages answer the core buyer questions in order: what MetasoftCo builds, who it builds for, what a participant experiences, what is custom, what is operationally scoped, what work proves the claim, and how to start a conversation.

No site-wide migration was performed. The new chrome and design system are activated only on the three approved English routes. Existing canonical URLs, hreflang pairs, English publication gates, sitemap logic and redirects remain in place.

## Recommended Positioning

### Primary brand category

**AI-powered experiential technology studio**

This has the best balance of commercial clarity, technical credibility, premium perception and future range. “Studio” supports bespoke creative work; “experiential technology” covers software plus physical execution; “AI-powered” names the current differentiating layer without reducing the company to an AI tool vendor.

### Supporting descriptor

**We design and build interactive AI experiences for brands, events and physical spaces—from concept and custom software to the live installation.**

### SEO descriptor

**AI event activation agency / experiential technology company**

These terms are plainer and closer to likely commercial search language than the brand category alone. Individual service pages should carry specific demand terms such as “AI photo booth for events.”

### Sales descriptor

**We build branded AI experiences people can step into, create with and share.**

### Core message

MetasoftCo combines **concept + technology + physical execution + branded participation**. The website should never imply that the company sells only software or commodity booth rental.

## Positioning Alternatives Considered

| Option | Strength | Limitation | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI-powered experiential technology studio | Accurate across creative, software and physical delivery; premium; expandable | Needs a plain-language supporting line | Recommended |
| AI event activation agency | Immediately commercial and search-friendly | Narrows the business to events and can feel execution-only | Use as SEO/sales language, not the brand category |
| Creative technology studio | Premium and internationally familiar | Does not identify the AI/event buying context quickly enough | Useful secondary association |
| AI-powered brand experience company | Buyer-friendly and broad | Less specific about technical build capability | Not selected |
| Experiential technology company | Clear and scalable | Less distinctive; removes a demonstrated AI strength | Strong fallback if AI becomes less central |

- Custom software and branded guest interfaces are present across the service catalogue and project records.
- Real-time image processing is demonstrated in the AI Photo experience.

- Branded outputs and QR-based digital delivery are documented in the selected service record.
- MetasoftCo can connect creative direction, interface, AI processing and physical event execution in one project.
- On-site installation/operation is represented in current company and service material.

### Potential positioning opportunities requiring proof

- Multi-country or multi-location deployment.
- Defined production speed or event throughput.
- Printing as a standard AI Photo capability rather than project-specific configuration.
- Lead capture, analytics and reporting.

These opportunities are not stated as facts in the prototype.

## Competitor Lessons

The market was reviewed again in August 2026 using current public sites.

- [Snapbar](https://snapbar.com) leads with measurable engagement and a clear Design → Access → Create → Measure flow. Lesson: connect the experience to the buyer’s campaign objective and make reporting proof visible when verified.
- [sharingbox](https://sharingbox.com) communicates end-to-end operational delivery, physical products and a global footprint. Lesson: operational proof can materially increase enterprise trust.
- [Noonah](https://noonah.com) directly addresses brands, events and agencies and balances in-house technology with physical/digital experiences. Lesson: name both the buyer and the delivery model early.
- [Snapmatic AI](https://www.snapmatic.ai) shows a focused AI Photo offer, sample outputs and a direct demo path. Lesson: service pages benefit from a tangible guest journey and immediate visual examples.
- [Primatix AI Photo Booth](https://www.primatix.co.uk/ai-photo-booth) uses before/after proof and brand recognition to make the service understandable quickly. Lesson: add structured input/output demonstrations when MetasoftCo has approved source assets.
- [UNIT9](https://www.unit9.com/work) is work-led, restrained and category-confident. Lesson: large project media can carry more authority than decorative effects.
- [Moment Factory](https://momentfactory.com) separates original properties from commissioned work and uses project scale to establish trust. Lesson: IA should eventually distinguish repeatable experiences from custom commissions.
- [Deeplocal](https://www.deeplocal.com/work) presents recognizable clients, years and compact project narratives. Lesson: case-study metadata and editorial consistency matter.

### Gap closed by the prototype

MetasoftCo now communicates its category, end-to-end role, participant experience and real work significantly faster. The work-first visual hierarchy is closer to premium creative-technology benchmarks without imitating them.

### Remaining competitive gap

The leading competitors still have stronger verified outcomes, operational scale data, public data/safety detail, client testimonials, case-study depth, service demos and multi-market proof.

## What Not to Copy

- Generic “innovation” language without an actual interaction or output.
- Demo-first SaaS framing that hides physical production.
- Commodity rental catalogues organised around equipment alone.
- Constant animated glow, parallax and AI-gradient decoration.
- Unverified counters, logo walls or global claims.
- Device mockups where live event photography is available.
- Aggressive forms before the visitor understands the work.

## Visual Direction

The direction is **editorial creative technology**:

- Near-black neutral backgrounds and restrained elevated surfaces.
- Warm-white typography with cyan used only for category labels, focus and the primary action.
- Oversized Space Grotesk display type, Manrope body copy and JetBrains Mono labels.
- Low-radius, line-based components instead of pill-heavy SaaS cards.
- Real project photography, installation media and AI outputs provide the colour.
- Motion remains purposeful; the homepage signal line and spectrum accents move continuously while the remaining prototype keeps media hover subtle. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Layout confidence comes from scale, spacing and asymmetry. Spectrum gradients are reserved for the homepage hero so they read as a branded signal rather than a site-wide effect.

## Hero Directions

### Direction 1 — Extreme clarity (implemented and recommended)

- Eyebrow: **AI-POWERED EXPERIENTIAL TECHNOLOGY**
- H1: **Interactive brand experiences, built from concept to show floor.**
- Support: MetasoftCo creates branded photo, video, game and installation experiences for brands and agencies—combining creative direction, custom software and live production.
- Primary CTA: **Plan Your Activation**
- Secondary CTA: **View Selected Work**
- Media: a real Ray-Ban participant image, supported by a Pegasus physical installation.
- Rationale: answers category, output, buyer and delivery model in the first viewport.
- Strongest audience: brand/marketing leads and agency producers comparing partners.

### Direction 2 — Premium creative technology

- Eyebrow: **CREATIVE TECHNOLOGY · LIVE EXPERIENCE**
- H1: **Technology people can step into.**
- Support: We turn campaign ideas into interactive AI, software and physical experiences for live brand environments.
- Primary CTA: **Explore the Work**
- Secondary CTA: **Plan Your Activation**
- Media: cinematic showreel alternating guests, installations and final outputs.
- Rationale: highest premium tone and strongest emotional economy, but needs the showreel to preserve clarity.
- Strongest audience: creative directors, innovation teams and global agency partners.

### Direction 3 — Proof/work-led

- Eyebrow: **SELECTED BRAND EXPERIENCES**
- H1: **See what happens when brands become interactive.**
- Support: AI portraits, games and installations designed, built and delivered by MetasoftCo.
- Primary CTA: **View Selected Work**
- Secondary CTA: **Plan Your Activation**
- Media: full-bleed project reel with named client/project captions.
- Rationale: puts evidence before the category claim and is strongest when the case-study library is complete.
- Strongest audience: agency creatives and visually led buyers.

Direction 1 was the initial prototype baseline because the current site needed category comprehension more urgently than additional mystique.

### Selected homepage revision — signal portal (implemented)

After visual review, the homepage hero was revised to the selected third visual concept while preserving the approved commercial explanation and conversion paths.

- H1: **EXPERIENCES THAT CONNECT BRANDS**.
- A cyan-to-violet-to-coral outline treatment gives “EXPERIENCES” a restrained animated spectrum signal.
- A real Ray-Ban participation image sits in a tall faceted portal with an animated gradient edge.
- The headline overlaps the live-work image to create one authored composition instead of a conventional split hero.
- The right-side buyer explanation and **Plan Your Activation / View Selected Work** actions remain explicit.
- The original continuously looping client-logo band sits directly below the hero; the interim AI Photo / Custom Software capability ticker has been removed.
- Mobile keeps the same hierarchy but removes horizontal stretching, stacks the media safely and preserves full-width conversion actions.

The composition responds to current experiential/creative-technology patterns observed at UNIT9, Active Theory, Resn, Lusion and FutureDeluxe: one confident media object, atmospheric depth and work-led visual authority. Snapbar's direct B2B explanation and CTA discipline remain in the copy block. The service and case-study prototypes were intentionally not changed.

### Full-page visual continuation (implemented)

The selected signal-portal language now continues through the complete homepage rather than stopping after the first viewport.

- Selected Work uses a large editorial heading, spectrum-outline emphasis, oversized project numbers and an asymmetric three-project composition.
- Capabilities becomes a luminous indexed system with larger outline numbers, stronger scan hierarchy and restrained hover movement.
- How We Build uses staggered steps, spectrum-outline numbers and a single continuous signal line.
- Why Interactive carries the cyan/coral atmospheric field into a value-led split layout with icon-supported proof points.
- The final CTA is now a homepage-specific signal field with an oversized outline statement and two explicit conversion paths.
- Desktop, tablet and mobile preserve the same section order and copy; only composition, scale and rhythm adapt.

The service and case-study routes remain unchanged. The broader homepage treatment is isolated through `p2-home-*` selectors so the accepted detail-page system does not inherit the new display scale or ambient fields.

## Case Study Architecture

Selected URL: `/en/projects/tavuk-dunyasi-x-ai-photo`

Tavuk Dünyası was selected because it passes the Phase 1 English publication gate and has a complete combination of recognizable client naming, main image, gallery, live vertical video, physical kiosk evidence, guest interaction and a direct link to the prototype service. Adidas has stronger brand recognition but its current English metadata is incomplete, so redesigning it would contradict the publication gate.

Reusable order:

1. Hero with client, format, year and output.
2. Opportunity rather than an invented “challenge.”
3. Concept and participant journey.
4. Physical execution and video proof.
5. Gallery of campaign outputs.
6. “What the project demonstrates” instead of unverified results.
7. Related capability.
8. Project CTA.

### Case-study CRO model

- Primary conversion: build a similar experience.
- Secondary conversion: open the related service or more work.
- Main objections: “Is the experience really live?”, “Is it custom to the brand?”, “What does the guest do?”, “What is the final output?”
- Proof required: kiosk video, campaign outputs, factual journey and named metadata.
- Missing proof: brief, venue/location, duration, audience volume, generated-output count, engagement, shares, testimonial and business result.

## Design System

## CTA System

- Preferred primary phrase: **Plan Your Activation**.
- Contextual case-study phrase: **Build a Similar Experience**.
- Primary style: cyan fill, dark text, 50px minimum height.
- Secondary style: transparent/dark surface with visible border.
- Text style: inline action with underline/border and arrow.
- Mobile: actions become full-width and retain at least 44px target size.
- Global floating chat and WhatsApp actions are hidden on the three prototypes to remove competition with the primary conversion.

## Content Voice

1. Lead with a concrete interaction or deliverable.
2. Name the buyer or environment when it adds clarity.
3. Use short, confident sentences and native international B2B English.
4. Explain the creative and technical layers in plain language.
5. Put evidence before adjectives.
6. Separate verified capability from per-brief scope.
7. Never imply scale, compliance, results or delivery geography without proof.

## Testing Results

- Production build: passed after implementation.
- TypeScript: passed with `tsc --noEmit`.
- Runtime database validation: passed as part of the production build.
- Responsive rendering: visually reviewed at desktop and approximately 390px.
- Overflow: tested at 320, 375, 430, 768 and 1440px on all three routes; no horizontal overflow detected.

- Accessibility basics: all three prototypes have one H1, no missing image alt attributes and no unlabelled buttons; visible focus, reduced motion and skip navigation are implemented. A formal assistive-technology audit remains a rollout gate.

## Recommendations Before Site-Wide Rollout

1. Validate positioning and CTA wording with sales and 3–5 target buyers.
2. Approve logo usage and prioritise a smaller, stronger global client set.
3. Complete English metadata/content for the strongest projects, especially Adidas, before migrating them.
4. Collect and legally approve operational, privacy and performance proof.
5. Add approved AI Photo input/output pairs and richer case-study source material.
6. Validate the design with a formal WCAG audit and representative keyboard/screen-reader checks.
7. Run production Lighthouse/WebPageTest against a deployable environment and set media budgets.
8. Confirm scalable CMS fields for opportunity, guest journey, technology, physical execution, results and metrics.
9. Adapt—not directly translate—the approved system into Turkish.
10. Migrate one content family at a time behind an explicit rollout plan and regression gate.

## Recommendation

**READY WITH MINOR CHANGES** for strategic and visual approval, not yet for immediate site-wide rollout. The system is strong enough to validate with buyers and stakeholders, but operational proof, case metrics, legal data language and final CTA/category choices should be confirmed before scaling it across the full site.
