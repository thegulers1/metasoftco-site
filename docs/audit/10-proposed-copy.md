# Proposed copy directions

These are strategic drafts, not final claims. Bracketed operating/geographic facts must be verified. Turkish and English should be edited as native market copy, not literal mirrors.

## Homepage direction 1 — measurable participation

Best for: brand marketing directors and experiential agencies evaluating business value.

**Eyebrow**  
AI-POWERED EXPERIENTIAL TECHNOLOGY

**H1**  
Experiences people step into. Results brands can build on.

**Supporting paragraph**  
MetasoftCo creates AI-powered photo, video, game and interactive installations for live brand experiences—combining creative concepting, custom software, physical production and on-site operation in one team.

**Primary CTA**  
Plan Your Activation

**Secondary CTA**  
View Case Studies

**Rationale**  
Balances human experience and commercial measurement, while making end-to-end scope explicit. It positions AI as a capability, not a novelty.

## Recommended direction

Direction 1 is the strongest default. It balances premium creativity, technology breadth and buyer outcomes without overcommitting to one operating model. Direction 2 can become a supporting “Why MetasoftCo” statement. Direction 3 is useful for campaign-focused pages.

## Trust copy modules

Only publish verified statements.

**End-to-end delivery**  
One team coordinates creative development, software, content, physical production and live operation—so the guest journey is tested as a whole before event day.

**Brand-safe AI**  
Creative styles, interface copy and final outputs are reviewed against the approved campaign direction. Moderation and fallback rules are agreed during production.

**Data and consent**  
Lead and image capture can be configured around the event brief, with clear consent, defined retention and post-event deletion/reporting workflows.

**Built for live conditions**  
Every activation is tested for the agreed venue, expected attendance and operating setup. Staffing, connectivity and fallback requirements are documented before deployment.

## Copy guardrails

- Do not call every format “unique,” “unforgettable,” “next-generation” or “revolutionary.”
- Do not lead with model names unless the buyer needs the distinction.
- Do not promise timing/accuracy/availability without scope and operational approval.
- Do not say “global” without naming a verified delivery model.
- Do not describe agencies/brands as direct clients when MetasoftCo was a subcontractor; credit relationships accurately.
- Do not translate `kirala` mechanically as `rental` on custom activation pages.
- Prefer one concrete noun/verb over stacked adjectives.
