## 2. Page-Type Alignment

- Target page type: **Service Page** — service descriptions, project cases, a four-step methodology, brand proof, and consultation CTAs.
- SERP expects: **Service Page / Hybrid**.
- Verdict: **ALIGNED**.
- Impact: no new page type is required for the broad brand/service query. The opportunity is to add decision-making depth and route high-intent users to a tailored brief/quote path.

## 3. User Stories from Search Signals

1. As a **brand marketing lead**, I want to see which AI experience fits my campaign, because I need a distinctive activation, but I am blocked by many similar-looking booth options.  
   Evidence: competitor result sections split AI/photo/game/software formats and repeatedly emphasize customization.

2. As a **budget-conscious event buyer**, I want to understand price drivers and get a fast quote, because I must secure budget approval, but I am blocked by absent pricing guidance.  
   Evidence: Fotokabin and Nox expose price/quote FAQs; Charlie's Event asks for budget range in its brief flow.

3. As a **technical event producer**, I want setup time, space, power, throughput, and onsite-support requirements, because venue failure is costly, but I am blocked by generic process copy.  
   Evidence: Nox's indexed FAQ answers area, electricity, installation time, and onsite operator questions.

4. As a **risk-averse enterprise buyer**, I want privacy, consent, references, and measurable outcomes, because brand and participant risk must be defensible, but I am blocked by claims that lack visible evidence.  
   Evidence: MetasoftCo's service snippet emphasizes KVKK; competitors emphasize client counts, testimonials, awards, and recognizable references.

5. As a **time-pressured planner**, I want a recommendation and response-time expectation, because my event date is fixed, but I am blocked by a generic contact handoff.  
   Evidence: Hatıra Atölyesi advertises response/setup timing; Charlie's Event offers an interactive recommendation flow.

### Weakest Persona: Procurement / budget buyer (57/100)

Top issue: the page offers “plan your activation” but no budget framework, package logic, or explanation of what changes the quote.

Recommended fix: add a “Teklifi neler belirler?” section immediately before the final CTA with event duration, location, participant count, station count, output type, branding, staffing, and logistics. Offer a short structured brief with budget range and event date.

## 6. Priority Actions

1. Add an above-the-fold choice path: “Deneyim önerisi al” and “Teklif iste,” each with event date, city, audience size, goal, and budget range.
2. Add a technical facts table covering setup time, footprint, power/internet, people/hour, staffing, output methods, and data-retention policy.
3. Add 6-8 visible FAQs matching price, setup, capacity, customization, service area, onsite support, and KVKK questions.
4. Add two or three testimonial/case-result cards with named role/company, measurable outcome, and linked project evidence.
5. Add crawlable video demos and `VideoObject`; connect services through `Service`/`OfferCatalog` schema.
6. Make the 100+ brands, 1,000+ live-event, and technical-accuracy claims auditable with dated methodology or case links.
7. Add updated dates to evergreen guides and project publication/event dates to case studies.
