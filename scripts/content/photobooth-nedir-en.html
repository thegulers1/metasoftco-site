What is a photo booth? A photo booth is an event activation where guests take their own photos at a kiosk made up of a camera, a screen and a printer, and get the result as a print and a digital copy within seconds. It is used everywhere from corporate events and trade shows to product launches and store openings. This guide explains what the word means, the main types of photo booth, how they work and how to choose the right one for your event.

What Does "Photo Booth" Mean?

A booth is a small enclosed space or stand; trade show stands are called booths too. The original photo booths were curtained cabins you stepped inside. Most photo booths rented for events today are open kiosks instead: guests do not go inside, they pose in front of the screen.

How Does a Photo Booth Work?

  1. Choose a mode: The guest picks photo, GIF or Boomerang on the touchscreen.
  2. Countdown and shot: They pose during the on-screen countdown; the camera and lighting work automatically.
  3. Editing: The photo is placed in a branded frame; if needed the background is replaced (green screen) or a filter is applied.
  4. Delivery: The photo is printed within seconds and also reaches the guest's phone via a QR code.

What Is a Photo Booth Used For at Events?

  • Engagement: Guests gather on their own, so the booth becomes a natural meeting point at the venue.
  • Keepsake: A printed photo carrying the brand's logo is a tangible memory guests take home.
  • Social media: Photos and GIFs that reach guests' phones by QR are shared during the event, giving the brand organic reach.
  • Data: An optional short form (with KVKK consent) can collect attendee details.

How to Choose the Right Photo Booth for Your Event

  1. Space: In tight spaces, a classic photo booth (about 2 m²); with more room, a 360 video booth (about 3 m²), a mirror booth (about 4 m²) or Cabin Photo (about 5 m²).
  2. Goal: If keepsakes and prints matter, choose a classic photo booth, a mirror booth or Cabin Photo; for social video, a 360 or GIF booth; for a brand story and a "wow" moment, an AI photo booth.
  3. Guest numbers: Per hour, a classic photo booth serves 70–80 guests (up to 150 with group shots), a mirror booth 60–80, Cabin Photo about 50, a 360 video booth 40–50 and a single-station AI photo booth 40–60. For large events, add stations.
  4. Branding: Ask whether the frame, welcome screen and kiosk wrap are designed for your brand.
